## Topics in this chapter

1. What an ORM is and why EF Core exists

5. Configuration: conventions, data annotations and Fluent API
6. Relationships: one-to-one, one-to-many and many-to-many
7. Change tracking and entity states
8. Tracking vs AsNoTracking
9. Loading related data: eager, explicit and lazy loading
10. The N+1 query problem and projection with Select
11. How LINQ is translated to SQL, and viewing the generated SQL
12. Single vs split queries
13. Raw SQL (FromSql, ExecuteSql) and parameterization
14. Bulk operations: ExecuteUpdate and ExecuteDelete
15. Transactions and SaveChanges
16. Optimistic concurrency with concurrency tokens
17. DbContext lifetime and thread safety
18. Connection pooling vs DbContext pooling
19. Global query filters (soft delete, multi-tenancy)
