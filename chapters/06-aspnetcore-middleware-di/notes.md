## Topics in this chapter

1. What ASP.NET Core is: hosting, Kestrel and Program.cs

3. Writing custom middleware and short-circuiting
4. Routing: controllers and actions vs Minimal APIs
5. Dependency injection: what it is and why it matters
6. Service lifetimes: Transient, Scoped and Singleton
7. Captive dependencies and lifetime mismatches (scoped inside singleton)
8. Keyed services (.NET 8) and factory registrations
9. Configuration sources and environments (appsettings, env vars, user secrets)
10. Options pattern: IOptions<T>, IOptionsSnapshot<T>, IOptionsMonitor<T>
11. HttpClient and IHttpClientFactory: named and typed clients
12. Middleware vs filters: when to use each (intro; filters in depth in Ch 8)
13. Application startup and graceful shutdown
